### Cross-cutting findings worth keeping

The cell notes surfaced concrete bugs/gaps in the framework that exist independently of the autopoiesis frame:

- **Two phantom scripts referenced everywhere.** `scripts/validate-identity.mjs` (referenced from `package.json:22` as `validate:schemas`) and `scripts/sync-upstream.mjs` (referenced from package.json, validate-structure.mjs, AGENTIC-ARCHITECTURE.md, FEDERATION.md, SKILL-PROMOTION.md, package-integration plan). - **Detection ≫ Propagation across the codebase.** `analyze-instances.mjs` detects drift; nothing corrects it. `validate-structure.mjs` checks invariants; nothing recovers when they break. The cognition loop has detection but the propagation script is missing.
- **No within-instance referential integrity check.** `data/skills-matrix.yaml` can list skills not on disk; no validator catches it.

- **Schema-on-read membrane is permeable.** Malformed YAML in `data/*.yaml` passes `validate:structure` (file exists) and silently drops rows in `generate:schemas`. External `.well-known/` lies about internal `data/`.
